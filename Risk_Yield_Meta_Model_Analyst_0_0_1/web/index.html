            How to read these indicators
            

              

                CUSUM Trend
                Detects persistent standardized shifts in the Hull-moving-average residual.
                Bands and trail use the price scale; candle tints and change markers describe
                an analytical state, not an order or fill.
              

              

                EWMA Volatility
                Shows close-to-close volatility per bar. Fast above slow suggests expansion;
                fast below slow suggests contraction. It measures risk magnitude, not direction.
              

              

                Vol-scaled Trend
                A bounded -1 to +1 directional composite. Sign indicates direction and path
                quality describes how cleanly price travelled; it is diagnostic evidence.
              

              

                Prototype Regime
                Shows fixed, untrained regime weights and change risk from 0% to 100%.
                Weights are not calibrated probabilities, confidence, or a trading gate.
              

              

                Cross-Timeframe Context
                Aligns the active timeframe with fixed higher-timeframe choices using only
                values already available after those candles closed. It describes trend
                alignment, conflict, or transition risk; it is not a trade instruction.
              

              

                Prior Structure
                Draws rolling highs and lows made only from bars available before the active
                candle. A test, rejection, or close beyond a prior level describes location
                and room-to-risk; it does not predict direction by itself.
              

              

                Comparable Volatility
                Uses the Rogers–Satchell OHLC range estimator with prior-only EWMA and rank
                references. In the fixed 0–100% pane, percentile is a rank and 50% is neutral
                for the signed ratio/shock scores. This is not direction or a cross-interval
                raw-vol comparison.
              

              

                Phase-adjusted Participation
                Compares finalized volume with prior observations from the same UTC/session
                phase. The fixed pane maps neutral activity to 50%; lower/higher values mean
                quieter/stronger participation. Futures, crypto, and FX-proxy volume units
                must not be compared directly.
              

              

                Timing: values use finalized candles and become actionable no
                earlier than the next bar. Prior channel lines are known at the active bar open;
                structure states, volatility, and participation are known after its close.
                Analytical events are separate from replay fills.
